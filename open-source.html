Audio engines, licenses and source

Local Music Organizer includes self-contained audio tools that run locally inside the application. They do not provide analytics, advertising or tracking and do not transmit the user's music library. No separate codec installation is required.

FFmpeg and FFprobe 7.1.1

Copyright FFmpeg contributors. Licensed under GNU LGPL 2.1 or later. The distributed build was configured without --enable-gpl and without --enable-nonfree. It is a static command-line build containing FFmpeg's built-in audio decoders, encoders, demuxers and muxers.
