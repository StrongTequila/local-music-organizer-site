Privacy Policy

Local Music Organizer processes music-library information and audio files locally on the user’s Mac.

Data collection

The application does not collect personal data, analytics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, usage history, contacts or location information. It does not create user accounts and does not use tracking technologies.

Local files and Music.app

The application accesses Music.app and files or folders selected by the user to provide organization, diagnostics, artwork inspection, conversion and maintenance features. Audio, metadata, artwork, playlists and conversion output remain on the user’s Mac and are not uploaded to the developer or third parties.

Third-party components

Bundled open-source audio engines run locally inside the application. They are not advertising or analytics SDKs and are not used to transmit user data.

Local storage and deletion

Preferences, folder permissions, the optional saved Library Index and optional logs are stored locally. Users can clear the saved index and logs in the application or remove the application and its local container data through macOS. The developer has no server-side user data to retain or delete.

Network access

Library analysis, audio inspection and conversion do not require a developer-operated service. Apple StoreKit communicates with Apple to start the free 3-day trial, complete the optional one-time Full Version purchase and restore purchases. The trial does not renew and creates no automatic charge. The developer does not receive Apple Account credentials or payment information. Links opened by the user open in the user’s web browser.
