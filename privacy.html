Local Music Organizer Privacy Policy

Effective July 12, 2026

Local Music Organizer processes music-library information and audio files locally on the user’s Mac.

Data collection

The application does not collect personal data, analytics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, usage history, contacts, or location information. It does not create user accounts and does not use tracking technologies.

Local files and Music.app

The application accesses Music.app and files or folders selected by the user to provide library organization, diagnostics, artwork inspection, conversion, and maintenance features. Audio, metadata, artwork, playlists, and conversion output remain on the user’s Mac and are not uploaded to the developer or third parties.

Third-party components

Bundled open-source audio engines run locally inside the application. They are not advertising or analytics SDKs and are not used to transmit user data.

Local storage and deletion

Preferences, folder permissions, optional caches, and optional logs are stored locally. Users can clear caches and logs in the application or remove the application and its local container data through macOS. The developer has no server-side user data to retain or delete.

Network access

The Mac App Store release does not require an internet connection for library analysis or conversion and does not contact an activation or licensing server. Links opened by the user, such as the website or a Discogs search, open in the user’s web browser.
